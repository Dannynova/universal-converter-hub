Frequently Asked Questions

The most common plot sizes in Nigeria are 50×100 ft (5,000 sq ft = 464.5 m²) and 60×120 ft (7,200 sq ft = 669 m²). Our calculator uses both standards.

1 acre = 43,560 sq ft. Dividing by 5,000 (50×100 ft plot) gives 8.71 plots; dividing by 7,200 (60×120 ft plot) gives 6.05 plots. Use the Acres to Plots calculator above.

1 hectare = 10,000 m². This equals approximately 21.53 plots of 50×100 ft, or 14.94 plots of 60×120 ft.
