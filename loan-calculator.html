Frequently Asked Questions

Select the Mortgage tab, enter the home price, down payment, interest rate and loan term, then click Calculate. The tool shows your monthly payment including principal, interest, taxes and insurance if entered.

An amortization schedule shows how each monthly payment is split between principal and interest over the life of the loan, and how your remaining balance decreases each month until it reaches zero at payoff.

The calculator uses the standard amortization formula: M = P[r(1+r)^n]/[(1+r)^n-1], where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of payments.
