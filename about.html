About ConverterHub

Free online conversion tools built specifically for Nigerian professionals — surveyors, engineers, educators, students and everyday users who need reliable tools that just work.

Our Mission

ConverterHub was created with one goal: give every Nigerian professional access to the same quality tools that were previously only available in expensive desktop software or unreliable third-party websites that compromise your data privacy.

Whether you are a surveyor converting NNO coordinates to UTM for a GIS report, a student compressing images for an assignment, a real estate agent calculating plot sizes, or a business owner translating a document — ConverterHub has a tool for you, completely free.

We believe that access to professional-grade tools should not be limited by budget or location. All tools are free, run in your browser, and respect your privacy.

Our Technology

ConverterHub is built entirely with standard HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no frameworks, no build tools, no server-side processing for most tools. This makes every tool fast to load, compatible with all modern browsers, and easy to maintain.

We use well-established open-source libraries where needed: pdf-lib and PDF.js for PDF processing, Mammoth.js for Word document rendering, Tesseract.js for OCR, proj4.js for geodetic coordinate transformation, SheetJS for spreadsheet conversion, and @imgly/background-removal for AI background removal.

The coordinate converter results are independently validated against Mobile Topographer Pro and SivanDesign — professional surveying tools used across Nigeria. The currency converter uses live data from ExchangeRate-API updated daily.

Privacy Commitment

We take data privacy seriously. The vast majority of our tools process all data locally in your browser — your documents, images and coordinates never leave your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

The only exception is the PDF-to-Word converter, which uses a secure third-party API. In this case, your file is transmitted securely over HTTPS, used only for the conversion, and deleted immediately. No file is ever stored beyond the conversion request. Please read our Privacy Policy for full details.
